Privacy Policy

MAT STORE uses customer data to operate secure commerce, remember region preferences, improve product discovery, and support marketplace automation.

Last updated May 26, 2026Passwords hashed with bcryptMAT AI enrichment
Privacy by architecture

Authentication, orders, carts, wishlists, and admin automation are separated behind backend services for cleaner future compliance work.

01

Account information

We store name, email, hashed password, country, currency, wishlist, saved addresses, and session records to provide account access and localized shopping.

02

Order information

Orders store customer contact details, shipping address, purchased items, totals, payment method, fulfillment status, and audit trail events.

03

Cart and recovery data

Cart records support checkout continuity and abandoned cart recovery logic. Recovery messages are email-ready but require a production email provider before launch.

04

MAT AI catalog data

When configured, private catalog metadata can be sent to MAT AI services to rewrite product titles, descriptions, SEO metadata, tags, and categories.

Security controls

Protected APIs require JWT authorization. Admin routes require the admin role. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Payment data

PayPal handles payment details through its secure checkout. MAT STORE stores order state and payment status, not full card details.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the contact page and include the email connected to your MAT STORE account.
